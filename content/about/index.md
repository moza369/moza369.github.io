## Journey

My interest in **cybersecurity** started before university, and it has remained one of the main areas I've continued to explore and develop over the years.

Before and during my university studies, I spent time learning about security through **practical experimentation, web application testing, CTFs, vulnerability research, and bug bounty hunting.** Over time, this developed into a stronger focus on offensive security and understanding how real-world applications and systems can be attacked.

My academic path in **software engineering** gave me a broader technical foundation in programming, databases, web technologies, and software development. Rather than being the beginning of my interest in security, it gave me additional technical knowledge that I could apply to the security work I was already interested in.

As I continued developing my skills, I expanded beyond web security and started exploring areas such as **Active Directory and Android security**, while continuing to focus primarily on web application security and vulnerability research.

I later chose to pursue a Master's in **Artificial Intelligence and Emerging Technologies (MIATE),** adding machine learning, computer vision, Big Data, cloud computing, and AI engineering to my technical background.

Today, my primary direction is **AI Engineering and Generative AI**, while my cybersecurity background continues to shape my interest in **AI Security** and secure AI systems.
